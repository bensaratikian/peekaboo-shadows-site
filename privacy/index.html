Introduction

Peekaboo Shadows has no accounts and never asks for your name, email or contacts; your game progress stays on your device. This policy explains the limited data the app and its service providers handle when you play.

Peekaboo Shadows ("the app") is published by Benik Saratikyan ("we", "us"). This policy covers the iPhone and iPad app. It does not cover other websites or services we link to.

Information the app collects

We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, photos or precise location.

How we use it

We use this information only to run, fund and improve the game. We do not sell it, and we do not use it to identify you.

  • Run the game: keep your progress, hints and settings on your device, and unlock purchases.
  • Improve it: see which pictures are too hard, where players get stuck and how hints are used.
  • Fix problems: find and fix crashes.
  • Show ads: serve, measure and limit how often ads appear, and prevent ad fraud.
  • Adjust settings remotely: turn ads or features on or off without an app update (Firebase Remote Config). This sends no personal data beyond the app-instance identifier.

Advertising and your choices

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob; you can limit how they use your data, or remove them.

  • Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland: before any ad loads, the app shows Google's consent form, where you choose whether your data may be used for personalised ads. Where the law requires it, you can change your choice later from Unlock All → Ad privacy choices.
  • No cross-app tracking: the app never asks for tracking permission, so ads are not personalised with your Advertising Identifier.
  • iOS settings: you can limit ad personalisation in Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising and Tracking.
  • Remove ads: the one-time Unlock All + No Ads purchase removes banner and between-picture ads. Hint videos stay available but only play when you choose to watch one.
  • Offline: without a connection no ads load and no data is sent; analytics events wait on the device until you are back online.

Children's privacy

Peekaboo Shadows is a general-audience game for families; it is not directed at children under 13 and is not in the App Store's Kids category.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Ads are limited to content rated suitable for general audiences. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has given us personal information, contact us (below) and we will delete it.

Data retention and security

Game data stays on your device until you delete the app; data held by Google is kept only as long as described below.

  • On your device: deleting the app removes your progress, hints and settings. Purchases can be restored from the App Store.
  • Analytics: Firebase keeps user-level analytics data for 2 months, after which only aggregated reports remain.
  • Crash reports: Crashlytics keeps crash reports for 90 days.
  • Advertising: Google AdMob keeps ad data under Google's retention policy.

Data sent to these services travels over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask us to access, correct or delete data about you, or object to how it is used; email us and we will reply within 30 days.

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland (GDPR / UK GDPR): you have the rights of access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability and objection, and you can withdraw consent at any time (Ad privacy choices, above). We rely on your consent for personalised ads and on our legitimate interest in running and improving the game for analytics and crash reports. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.
  • US states with privacy laws (for example California, Colorado, Virginia): you can request access to or deletion of your data. We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising using your Advertising Identifier.

Because we do not know who you are, we may need the app-instance identifier to find your data; we can send instructions on how to find it.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles data, we will update this page and its "Last updated" date before the change ships in an app update. Significant changes will also be noted in the App Store release notes.
