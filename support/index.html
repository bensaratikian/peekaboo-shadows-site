Support

Peekaboo Shadows for iPhone and iPad

Questions, a bug, or a picture you're stuck on? Email benik.saratikyan@gmail.com. Mention your device and iOS version if something isn't working.

How to play

Drag the toy to turn it, and drag up or down to tip it. When its shadow on the wall matches the picture, it snaps into place. On pictures with two pieces, tap a piece to choose it, then press and hold to slide it.

Common questions

  • I'm stuck. Tap the lightbulb to see the picture's outline on the wall. You start with 5 hints and get 3 more for every world you finish; with none left you can watch a short video for one, or buy a pack.
  • Restore a purchase. Open the picture menu, tap Unlock All, then Restore purchase, signed in with the Apple ID you bought it with.
  • Remove ads. The one-time Unlock All + No Ads purchase opens every world and removes banner and between-picture ads.
  • Change ad privacy choices. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland: Unlock All → Ad privacy choices.
  • Videos or purchases don't work. They need an internet connection; the game itself plays offline.
